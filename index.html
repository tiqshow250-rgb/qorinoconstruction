Executive Snapshot

A startup entering at exactly the right moment.

Global construction value added is projected at US$6.56 trillion in 2026, with a CAGR of 5.34% expected through 2027. Output is forecast to expand by 0.8% in real terms in 2026 after a 0.3% contraction in 2025 — and South Asia is growing at 6.0%, the fastest region in the world. Qorino enters during a recovery phase, when competition for talent and subcontract capacity is softer.

◆

Our Entry Strategy

We enter through specialised, technology-enabled niche services — site preparation, concrete works, steel erection support and BIM coordination — rather than competing head-on with established mega-contractors on day one.

◈

Our Scale-Up Path

Subcontracting and site services in Year 1 → mid-size general contracting in Years 2–3 → selective mega-project participation from Year 4. Crawl, walk, run — deliberately sequenced.

▣

Our Technology Edge

BIM + Lean Construction from the very first project. Drones, layout robots and reality capture before heavy robotics. We rent advanced plant rather than sinking early capital into a fleet.

Strategic Recommendation

Begin with drones, layout printing robots and reality capture — low capital, high ROI. Add autonomous earthmoving via rental or lease on larger sites as the company scales. Do not attempt to own a full robotics fleet in Year 1.

Human Power

Lean core. Deep craft. Real capability.

Construction occupations span from project professionals to craftsmen, operators and labourers. Qorino's workforce plan follows a three-tier pyramid — heavy on delivery, light on overhead.

◉

Tier 1 — Leadership & Professionals

10–15% of headcount. Construction managers, project managers, site engineers, planning engineers, quantity surveyors, HSE managers, BIM coordinators, contract administrators, architects (liaison), structural and MEP engineers.

◈

Tier 2 — Skilled Trades & Technicians

50–60% of headcount. Concrete finishers, steel fixers, carpenters, formwork carpenters, electricians, plumbers, HVAC technicians, welders, riggers, crane and excavator operators, survey and laboratory technicians, scaffolders.

▤

Tier 3 — Labour & Support

25–35% of headcount. Construction labourers, helpers, flaggers, cleaners, storekeepers, security, drivers and catering staff.

Human Resource Challenges

Talent Competition

Established contractors offer stability. Qorino must compete on culture, equity and project variety.

Certification Gaps

Skilled trades require verified certifications — OSHA 30, crane operator licences, welding certificates.

Cash Flow & Payroll

Construction faces long payment cycles, making payroll continuity a critical risk. Prioritise projects with milestone-based payment terms.

Retention

Invest in training pathways and clear progression from labourer to technician to supervisor.

Six Internal Policies from Day One

01

Zero-Harm Safety

No task is urgent enough to compromise safety. Daily toolbox talks, weekly inspections, monthly audits.

02

Digital-by-Default

All site records, permits, inspections and handover documents live in a cloud-based Common Data Environment.

03

Ethical Procurement

Transparent supplier selection, no facilitation payments, mandatory conflict-of-interest declarations.

04

Environmental Management

Aligned with ISO 14001 principles from day one — waste segregation, dust suppression, noise monitoring.

05

Quality Management

ITP (inspection and test plans) for every work package; third-party testing for concrete and steel.

06

Subcontractor Prequalification

Mandatory HSE record, insurance and financial checks before any subcontract award.

Brand Identity

The Q-Block mark.

A geometric monogram Q in structural blue, with a three-tiered building form in safety orange replacing the traditional tail. The gap between the circle and the building forms a subtle upward arrow — growth and forward motion.

Design Rationale

The circle represents the Q's bowl and the precision of engineering. The three-tiered orange building rising from the lower-right quadrant replaces the Q's tail — a skyscraper silhouette, a rising city block, a built future. The negative space between the two forms a quiet upward arrow. The mark reads instantly at hard-hat sticker size and holds up on site hoardings, app icons and document templates alike.

Wordmark

QORINO in a bold, extended sans-serif (Inter Black or Montserrat ExtraBold), letter-spaced at 0.09em. CONSTRUCTION below in a lighter weight, tracked out at 0.34em in safety orange.
